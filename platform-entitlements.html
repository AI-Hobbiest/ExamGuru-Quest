Entitlement principles

  • Platform Admin is the only role that grants or revokes subscriptions.
  • Institution Admin can assign entitled content to its own learners, but cannot expand entitlements.
  • Entitlements are explicit and class/exam scoped.
  • Direct Learning receives the supported curriculum classes by default.
  • Current implementation stores these settings locally; cloud persistence and server-side enforcement are a later migration stage.
