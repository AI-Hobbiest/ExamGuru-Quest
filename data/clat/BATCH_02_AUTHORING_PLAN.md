# CLAT UG 2027 — Batch 02 Production Plan

**Batch:** Papers 006–015  
**Target:** 10 full-length papers / 1,200 original questions  
**Blueprint:** CLAT-UG27-v1  
**Status:** Papers 006–010 are committed as unpublished drafts and pass strict structural CI; Papers 011–015 remain planned. No Batch 02 paper is student-facing or editorially approved.

## Non-negotiable paper blueprint

- 120 questions; 120 minutes; +1 correct, −0.25 incorrect, 0 unanswered.
- English: 24; Current Affairs & GK: 30; Legal Reasoning: 30; Logical Reasoning: 24; Quantitative Techniques: 12.
- Original passages and datasets; 4 options; one defensible key; explanatory rationale; difficulty and competency on every question.
- Exact passage and question uniqueness against Papers 001–005 and within Batch 02.
- Balanced answer positions and realistic difficulty; independent recalculation of every QT answer.
- Current Affairs: dated passages, source title, publisher, date and HTTPS primary/high-authority source; no unsupported or stale claims.

## Batch-wide content direction

Batch 01 already covered many PIB announcements and Indian administrative releases. Batch 02 must widen the reading mix: international institutions and law, global environmental governance, science and technology, demographic change, labour, culture, public policy and international sport. Questions should test what a careful CLAT candidate can infer from the passage, not simply extract isolated facts.

## Acceptance and release sequence

1. Draft the complete paper and freeze its passage/question IDs.
2. Run strict blueprint, word-band, source-metadata, option-distinctness, key-balance and difficulty checks.
3. Independently recalculate every Quantitative Techniques answer.
4. Check exact stems/passages and review semantic overlap against Papers 001–005 and earlier Batch 02 drafts.
5. Run a blind solve and ambiguity review; correct and re-run all checks.
6. Only then append the paper to the student practice-pilot library. Draft files remain unpublished.

The ten papers will be committed as separate draft JSON files, then released together only after the entire batch-level integrity test passes. This plan is a production map, not a claim that any of Papers 006–015 has been completed.
