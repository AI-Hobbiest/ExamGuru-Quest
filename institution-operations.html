Independent and entrance-exam learners

These learners still belong to an organisational tenant, but not necessarily a school. ExamGuru Direct Learning is available as a virtual institution. Create a learning cohort such as “CLAT UG 2027” or “Independent Class 10” under Academic Structure, then assign the learner to that cohort. This preserves one identity and reporting model.
