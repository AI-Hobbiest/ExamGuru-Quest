🎒 Student Guide

1. Sign in

  1. Open the ExamGuru Quest sign-in page and enter the username and password provided by your parent, teacher or administrator.
  2. Use your own account. Your class controls which subjects and examination routes are shown.
  3. If sign-in fails, check spelling and capitalisation, then ask your parent/teacher to confirm the account is active. Do not share your password with classmates.

2. Understand your dashboard

Your dashboard is your learning home. It shows your available subjects, learning level, XP, achievements and activity. Subject cards show the MCQ practice availability for that subject; they are not a count of every question format held by the school.

3. Practise a subject

  1. Choose a subject card or open a subject from the navigation.
  2. Select the chapter or topic and the practice mode offered.
  3. Read each question carefully, select an answer and use the available navigation controls.
  4. Review the explanation and result after submitting. Use mistakes and bookmarks in the Learning Hub for revision where available.

4. Take assigned tests

Open Assigned tests from your dashboard. Read the title, subject, duration and instructions before starting. Begin only when you have enough uninterrupted time to finish.

5. Review results

After a submitted test, review your score, accuracy and feedback. Your profile keeps your available test history and learning progress on the browser/device where you worked.

Important: At this stage, learning history is stored locally in the browser/device. It does not automatically synchronise between a phone, tablet and laptop. Use the Profile backup and restore tools when moving data between devices.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent & Teacher Guide

1. Open the Family Dashboard

Sign in with the parent/guardian account. The Family Dashboard lists only the student accounts linked to that parent account. If no child appears, ask the administrator to check the family link.

2. Review the family overview

Each child card summarises completed tests, average accuracy, current streak, subjects practised, learning level/XP and recent activity. Select a child's card to open that child's detailed view.

3. Switch between children

Return to the family cards and select another child. The detail view should change to the selected child's name, class, subject performance, learning insights and chapter progress. Always check the child name at the top before reviewing or printing a report.

4. View and save a progress report

  1. Open the intended child's detail view.
  2. Choose Print / Save PDF report.
  3. In the browser print screen, select a printer or Save as PDF, then save the file.
  4. On a phone or tablet, use the system print/share options offered by the browser. If no print window appears, allow pop-ups for the ExamGuru site and retry.

Reports include completed-test totals, average accuracy, XP/level, subject performance, learning insights, consistency and chapter progress where the attempt data supports it. Full papers awaiting manual marking are excluded from score averages until evaluated.

5. Parent/Teacher assessment tools

Use the parent workspace's assessment area to review available test and paper options. Read the assessment settings and preview the paper before assigning or sharing it with a student.

Privacy note: Parent view is read-only for student learning records. Reports reflect progress stored on the current browser/device; they are not a cloud-synchronised family record yet.

📝 Examination Instructions

Before you begin

  • Check the subject, class, paper title, question count, maximum marks and duration.
  • Keep a stable internet connection and sufficient battery. Close unrelated tabs and apps.
  • For a printable paper, read the cover instructions and ensure you have the required writing materials.

During an online test

  • Read the full question and all options before answering.
  • Use the question navigator and Previous/Next controls where available. If the interface allows answer changes before submission, confirm the selected option shown on screen.
  • Watch the timer. The countdown indicates the remaining time; do not assume that refreshing the page will pause or reset it.
  • Do not close the tab, clear browser data or sign out while an attempt is in progress.

Submission and interruption

  • Review unanswered questions if the test provides a review screen, then use the final Submit action.
  • Wait for the result/confirmation screen before closing the page.
  • If the browser closes, the device loses power or the connection drops, reopen the same site and sign in again. Check whether the attempt is still available or already submitted.
  • If the attempt is missing, duplicated or stuck, note the test name, approximate time and device/browser, then contact the teacher/administrator. Do not repeatedly start new attempts while the status is unclear.
Exam integrity: Work independently, do not use outside help during a timed examination, and follow any additional instructions provided by your teacher.

❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Check the username and password carefully, including capital letters. Confirm that you are using the correct student or parent account. If it still fails, ask the administrator to verify or reset the account.

Why can I see only some subjects?

Subject availability depends on the class and the subjects configured for that class. Ask your teacher/administrator if you believe a subject is missing.

Why does the student dashboard show MCQ counts only?

Those counts describe the MCQ practice bank available to students. Other question formats and the full authoring inventory are administrative information and are not shown in the student dashboard.

Why is a completed test not appearing in another device?

Progress is currently stored locally in the browser/device. It does not automatically sync between devices. Use Profile → Backup & restore to export and import a backup when needed.

The Parent PDF report did not open. Is the report broken?

The browser may have blocked the new report window. Allow pop-ups for the ExamGuru site, retry the report button, and then use Print / Save as PDF in the report tab. On iPhone/iPad, use the browser's print or share workflow.

How do I make sure I am viewing the correct child?

Return to the Family Dashboard, select the child's card and verify the child's name and class in the detail heading before opening a report.

The timer is running and I lost connection. Will it pause?

Do not assume the timer pauses. Reopen the site, sign in and check the attempt status. If the attempt is not recoverable, contact your teacher/administrator with the test name and approximate interruption time.

Can I change an answer after moving to the next question?

Use the Previous/Next or question navigator controls if they are available in that test. The selected answer shown by the interface is the one to verify before final submission. Some paper or test formats may have different navigation rules.

Why is my full-paper result not included in my score?

Some printable or hybrid papers require manual marking. They are excluded from score averages until the teacher completes evaluation and records the result.

Who should I contact for help?

Contact your parent, class teacher or ExamGuru administrator. Include your username (never your password), class, page/test name, device and browser, what you expected, and what happened.
