Using CBSE paper packages

CBSE currently distributes these question papers and marking schemes as subject-wise ZIP packages, rather than individual PDF URLs. Select a card to download the package, extract it on your device, then open the PDF included inside. On iPhone/iPad, use the Files app to tap the downloaded ZIP and extract it.

Official Marking Scheme: each card provides a separate download when CBSE lists one. Each card is built from a subject/year entry listed in the CBSE archive. The question paper and official marking scheme are separate downloads. Where CBSE combines subjects in one package, the card says so. If a scheme is unavailable in the verified catalogue, ExamGuru will say that rather than display an incorrect key.

2022 entries are Term II papers; 2021 regular Class X board examinations were cancelled, so no normal 2021 paper is listed.

Source references: CBSE Question Paper Archive · CBSE Marking Scheme Archive. ExamGuru links to original CBSE-hosted packages and does not rehost or modify them.
