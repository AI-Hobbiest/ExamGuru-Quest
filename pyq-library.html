How to open the exact paper and answer key

  1. Choose a subject and examination year above.
  2. Open the official CBSE question-paper archive. In the Class X section, select the matching year and subject.
  3. Open the official marking-scheme archive separately and select the same examination year and subject. CBSE may publish marking schemes for only some subject/year combinations.

Important: These are official archive links, not ExamGuru-created answer keys. A marking scheme may not be available for every paper. For descriptive subjects, use the official marking scheme rather than an unofficial answer list.

2022 was a split-term examination year. Check whether the downloaded paper is Term I or Term II before attempting it. The 2021 regular Class X board examination was cancelled and is not listed as a completed board-paper year.
